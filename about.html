About

SmallBiz Site Guide exists because most advice about small business websites is written by people selling websites.

Search for what a site should cost and you will find page after page produced by agencies, each ending in a request for a quote. The numbers are vague, the scope is unclear, and the point is never to help you decide — it is to get you on a call.

This site does the opposite. Every guide answers one question directly, gives you a real number, and says plainly when the cheaper option is the better one. If the honest answer is "you do not need to pay for that," that is what the page says.

How it is funded

Some pages contain affiliate links to tools and services. If you buy through one, this site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That never changes the recommendation — programs that do not fit are left out, and the cheapest sensible option is recommended even when it pays nothing. The full explanation is on the disclosure page.

Corrections

Prices and platform terms change. Every page carries the date its figures were last checked. If you spot something that is out of date, please say so and it will be fixed.
